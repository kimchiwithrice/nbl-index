About {{ cfg.site_name }}

{{ cfg.site_name }} is a free, independent stats site for the NBL, in the spirit of KenPom and Bart Torvik's T-Rank in US college basketball. It rates every team on adjusted offense, defense and tempo, predicts every game, simulates the season, and tracks its own accuracy in public.

It's built and run by one fan. It's not affiliated with the NBL or any club.
