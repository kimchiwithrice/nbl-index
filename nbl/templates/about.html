About {{ cfg.site_name }}

{{ cfg.site_name }} is a free stats site for the NBL. If you know KenPom or Bart Torvik's T-Rank from US college hoops, it's that idea pointed at our league: team ratings adjusted for schedule, a prediction for every game, the season simulated, and a public record of how the model's actually going.

It's a one-person side project, built because I wanted NBL numbers like this in one place. It isn't connected to the NBL or any club.

Spoiler-free mode

Watching on replay? Hit Hide scores at the top of any page and scores, records and results disappear across the whole site. Played games just get a watchability rating instead. It remembers your choice on that device.

Credits

The data comes from the NBL's official stats feed through Jason Zivkovic's open-source nblR project. The method borrows heavily from Ken Pomeroy and Dean Oliver.
