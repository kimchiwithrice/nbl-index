Glossary

New here? Start with the team ratings, League today or the NBL advanced stats. This page explains what every number on the site means and how it's worked out. In the formulas, FGM/FGA are field goals made/attempted, 3PM is threes made, FTA is free-throw attempts, ORB/DRB are offensive/defensive rebounds and TOV is turnovers.
